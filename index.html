Plant care basics

☼

Light

Most houseplants prefer bright, indirect light. A sheer curtain protects from harsh afternoon sun.

💧

Water

Overwatering kills more plants than anything else. Check the top 2cm of soil before watering.

🪴

Soil

Most tropicals need well-draining mix. Add perlite and repot every 1–2 years.

🌫

Humidity

Tropical plants love moisture. Group plants together or use a pebble tray in dry winters.
